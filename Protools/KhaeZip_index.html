KhaeZip Safety Guide

  • Keep an original copy of irreplaceable files outside any new archive.
  • Encrypted KhaeZip files require the exact password, including spaces.
  • Split archives need every numbered part. Keep the verification manifest with them.
  • Integrity Scan verifies CRC-32 after extraction. Repair never alters the source archive.
  • This edition intentionally rejects ZIP64-scale or suspiciously large jobs.
