Privacy First

KhaeZip performs archive work inside this browser. It does not upload files, contact an archive service, load a CDN, transmit passwords, or synchronize the local vault. Browser downloads and IndexedDB records remain under the control—and storage policies—of this device and browser profile.

KhaeZip Safety Guide

  • Keep an original copy of irreplaceable files outside any new archive.
  • Encrypted KhaeZip files require the exact password, including spaces.
  • Split archives need every numbered part. Keep the verification manifest with them.
  • CRC Integrity Scan verifies ZIP checksums. Deep SHA-256 Scan also validates KhaeZip’s internal manifest when present.
  • This edition intentionally rejects ZIP64-scale, overlapping, path-traversal, suspiciously large, or malformed archive structures.
