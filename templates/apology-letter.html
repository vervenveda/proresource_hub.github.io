Apology Letter

Dear [Name],

Acknowledgment

I am sorry for [specific action, words, delay, omission, or harm].

Responsibility

[State what you did without minimizing, blaming, or centering your intent over the impact.]

Impact

[Acknowledge what the other person may have experienced or lost.]

Repair

[State what you will do to correct, replace, repay, clarify, protect, or prevent recurrence.]

Change

[Explain the concrete action, boundary, practice, or learning you are adopting.]

Respect for Their Response

[Allow the recipient time and choice. Do not demand forgiveness.]

Closing

[Offer a sincere final apology and appropriate next step.]

Sincerely,

[Your Name]
